Stop right away if

  • You feel chest pain or tightness
  • You feel dizzy or light-headed
  • Breathlessness doesn't settle within 2–3 minutes

Before starting, get BP, blood sugar (HbA1c), a lipid profile and an ECG checked.

Training rules

  • Breathe out as you push or pull. Never hold your breath.
  • Pick a weight where the last 2 reps are hard but form stays clean.
  • When all sets feel smooth, add a little weight next session.
  • Missed a day? Continue from the next day. Never miss two in a row.

Never on the menu

  • Sugar in chai or coffee, cold drinks, packaged juice
  • Samosa, vada pav, bhaji, namkeen, biscuits
  • Mithai and bakery items, except the one weekly treat from week 9
  • Alcohol. It goes straight to the belly.
