                Start fresh

                Erases every log: workouts, sets, food, water, quests, body log, rank tests, Mission progress and XP. Your settings (height, age, diet, asthma mode) stay. A backup file is saved to Downloads first, just in case.

                Send your data to Claude (or a spreadsheet) to check what is working. Includes food and macros per day, workouts, sets, cardio, core, mobility, breathing, water, waist and weight, rank tests and Mission hours.

                Backup

                Safety

                • Stop right away if you feel chest pain or tightness, dizziness, or breathlessness that doesn't settle within 2–3 minutes.
                • Get BP, blood sugar (HbA1c), a lipid profile and an ECG checked before starting.
                • Heavy barbell work: always set safety pins or use a spotter.
